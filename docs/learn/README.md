# Learn: swarms, shared intelligence, and trust

New here? Start here. No engineering background needed.

A flock of birds turns as one, in a blink. No bird is in charge, no
bird sees the whole sky, and no human watching could make those
decisions that fast - our senses overload following even one or two
birds. Flockinator rebuilds that puzzle with sixteen small robot
panels so we can slow it down, measure it, attack it, and learn how
groups become smarter than their members - and how that smartness
can be stolen. The same channels that make a group capable also
make it attackable; that is why trust is tested, not assumed.

## The seven ideas

1. **[Why groups look intelligent](why-groups-look-intelligent.md)**
   Simple local rules, repeated across many neighbours, create
   patterns nobody programmed.
2. **[Intelligence between nodes](extra-node-intelligence.md)**
   Some knowledge does not live inside any single node. It lives in
   who trusts whom, what gets copied, and what the group refuses to
   believe. Nature may use this to store information cheaply -
   in the pattern, not the individual - so it can survive
   generations of short-lived members.
3. **[Trust, scepticism, and attackers](trust-and-attackers.md)**
   Shared intelligence only works if communication can be trusted -
   or at least doubted well. Attackers will try to impersonate,
   copy, delay, and mislead the swarm. Defending means preparing
   for attacks we can imagine, and staying sceptical about ones
   we cannot.
4. **[Authority: the commander and the platoon](authority-and-trust.md)**
   Why would a commander deploy such a swarm? For the same reason
   a commander trusts a platoon: each soldier must make good
   decisions alone, the group must make better ones together, and
   that trust is earned by testing - friendly and hostile - long
   before deployment.
5. **[Why search and rescue first](why-search-and-rescue.md)** - lost
   children, downed aircraft, thermal and CO2 scouts, and why
   agent-to-agent trust is a new frontier.
6. **[Optimisation in nature](optimisation-in-nature.md)** - gradient descent without a gradient; survivors, not the smartest.
7. **[How Flockinator tests all this](how-we-test.md)**
   The playground: sixteen glowing nodes, a search-and-rescue
   mission, an adversary whose job is to lie to them, and a
   scorekeeper with the hidden truth.
