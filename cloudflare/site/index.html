Flockinator - a microscope for emergent behaviour

Sixteen embodied agents, staged in sound, light, and language on a wall of nodes, so humans can spot what emerges between agents - then prove or refute the spot against sealed evidence.

Why this is useful: we experiment with intelligent agents to identify emergent behaviour — what a group does that no single agent was programmed to do. The point is practical: edge devices such as search-and-rescue quadcopters must act autonomously in the real world, where communications fail and sensors lie. A swarm that has learned when to trust, when to corroborate, and when to abstain is safer to deploy than one that has only ever flown in friendly conditions.

Interactive simulation only. Nothing here controls real hardware.
